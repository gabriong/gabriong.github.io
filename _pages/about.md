I have a strong interest in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Synthetic Biology, and Chemical Biology, with a core focus on computational protein design. My aim is to develop novel enzymes and molecular systems that address critical challenges in sustainability, such as advancing green chemistry and enabling bio-based manufacturing.

My work integrates methods and insights across AI, biology, and chemistry to reimagine how we understand and engineer proteins. With a strong foundation in interdisciplinary research and systems thinking, I strive to bridge the gap between academic discovery and industrial application, creating solutions that are both scientifically groundbreaking and practically impactful.

I am a recipient of the A*STAR National Science Scholarship, one of Singapore's most prestigious awards for scientific research, which supports my PhD training and continues to inspire my commitment to translational science.
